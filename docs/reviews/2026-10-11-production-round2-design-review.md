### R2F1 [P2] Duplicate Native Formatters Cross Launch Ownership (V1; Resolved In V2)

Sources: [context.rb:58](/private/tmp/reportportal-round2-master-20261011/frozen-source/lib/reportportal_cucumber/runtime/context.rb:58), [context.rb:484](/private/tmp/reportportal-round2-master-20261011/frozen-source/lib/reportportal_cucumber/runtime/context.rb:484), [context.rb:489](/private/tmp/reportportal-round2-master-20261011/frozen-source/lib/reportportal_cucumber/runtime/context.rb:489), [formatter.rb:656](/private/tmp/reportportal-round2-master-20261011/frozen-source/lib/reportportal_cucumber/cucumber/formatter.rb:656).

Two actual native formatters in one Cucumber runtime have separate launch UUIDs but share the thread-wide context stack/state. Native AppLog evidence delivered through the first formatter consequently pairs its launch UUID with an action UUID created under the second launch. This is not merely duplicate logging or a synthetic lifecycle event.

- Fresh installed Cucumber 10.2.0 and 9.2.1: two sequential duplicate-formatter runs followed by an unformatted run execute business code and native hooks in three distinct registries/event buses. - Example installed C10 wire: item `2e1d1a58-cf00-4597-9b8c-ddea3a28150a` was created under launch `99c15561-94ed-4dae-a802-637db1539e70`, but its first-formatter log specifies launch `1ed29da8-141d-41cd-bb4a-d3e61844f895`.
- Reproduction: [native_matrix.rb](/private/tmp/reportportal-round2-review-20261011/native_matrix.rb) registers the real formatter twice; [ownership_reconcile.rb](/private/tmp/reportportal-round2-review-20261011/ownership_reconcile.rb) joins log ownership against actual captured item-start requests.
