ReportPortal rich-experience chart was rendered.

Official ReportPortal is installed as the `reportportal` dependency.

For Ruby video playback mode, configure test runners with:

  RP_VIDEO_UPLOAD_MODE=minio_markdown
  RP_MINIO_ENDPOINT={{ .Values.videoBucket.endpoint.scheme }}://{{ include "reportportal-rich-experience.minioHost" . }}:{{ .Values.videoBucket.endpoint.port }}
  RP_MINIO_PUBLIC_BASE_URL=https://<reportportal-ingress-host>
  RP_MINIO_BUCKET={{ .Values.videoBucket.name }}
  RP_MINIO_ACCESS_KEY_ID=<{{ include "reportportal-rich-experience.videoBucketSecretName" . }}:{{ .Values.videoBucket.auth.accessKeyKey }}>
  RP_MINIO_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY=<{{ include "reportportal-rich-experience.videoBucketSecretName" . }}:{{ .Values.videoBucket.auth.secretKeyKey }}>
  RP_MINIO_REGION=us-east-1

The extra Ingress routes {{ .Values.videoBucket.ingress.path }} to service
{{ include "reportportal-rich-experience.minioServiceName" . }}:{{ .Values.videoBucket.ingress.servicePort }}
without rewriting the path, so path-style MinIO URLs keep the bucket name.
